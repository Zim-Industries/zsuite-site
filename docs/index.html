CURRENT CAPABILITIES

Timekeeping and
field operations.

Employees record work in Field. Office teams review time, manage schedules and maintain the records that support each job.

01 / TIME

Time cards and
payroll exports.

Employees clock time to a project and cost code, check their cards and request corrections. Reviewers approve or return cards, resolve exceptions and close the period for payroll export.

  • Online clock-in/out, breaks and work-allocation changes
  • Individual and bulk time-card approval, returns and corrections
  • Period readiness, locking and mapped payroll CSV exports

Payroll handoff uses files. Z-Suite does not issue wages, make direct deposits or file payroll taxes.

02 / PEOPLE

Schedules and
employee records.

Office teams schedule crews and maintain employee records. Employees view published shifts, acknowledge assignments and request time off. Attendance views compare scheduled work with clocked time.

  • Day, week and month planning with recurring events
  • Employee assignments, shift acknowledgements and time off
  • Crews, positions, employment history and scoped roles

Attendance comparisons are separate from approved payroll time. Scheduling does not include construction critical-path planning or automatic optimization.

03 / EQUIPMENT

Equipment bookings
and service history.

The equipment record holds service history, inspections, defects and booking information. Office teams review operator qualifications and can link an asset to a selected shift or time entry.

  • Registry, asset history, files, custody and manual locations
  • Maintenance, service, meters, inspections and defects
  • Requests, bookings, operator reviews and work associations
  • Read-only Daily JSA checks on registered assets, linked to saved source revisions

JSA checks require whole-company Equipment and Daily JSA read access; typed unit numbers are not matched. This scope does not include live GPS/telematics, parts inventory or automatic equipment usage costing.

04 / FIELD RECORDS

Forms, files and
daily reports.

Employees complete forms with answers, photos, files or signatures. Office reviewers inspect submissions and their supporting evidence. Shared Files holds project photos and documents alongside daily-report and expense workflows.

  • Forms with photos, files, signatures and review controls
  • Office daily reports, coverage views and finalization
  • Shared Files, expenses, per diem and reimbursement handoffs

Uploads depend on configured storage and scanning. Reimbursement records document external payments; Z-Suite does not transfer funds.

05 / SAFETY & TRAINING

Daily JSAs and
training records.

Crew leads prepare daily job safety analyses (JSAs) with hazards, controls and sign-ins. Office users review signed content, record permitted corrections and generate PDFs. Training records track sessions, completions, renewals and expiry.

  • Field daily JSA capture, hazards, controls, sketches and sign-ins
  • Office search, record review, managed corrections and late sign-ins
  • Formatted PDF/print and on-demand project bundles of up to 31 days
  • Optional NWS forecast-high and EPA AirNow PM2.5 AQI draft pre-fill; values remain editable
  • Reusable JSA libraries, observations, training and qualification evidence

Bundles cover one project, exclude drafts/archives and require whole-company JSA read or manage access. Lookup needs provider setup, a published site boundary and supported US-provider coverage; manual entry remains available. AirNow requires a key and validation on its first deployment; this guide does not verify live lookup. Some other Office safety masters remain read-only. Safety records do not certify regulatory compliance or guarantee safe work.

PRODUCT SCOPE & DIRECTION

What the platform does today.
Where it goes next.

Records linked to the work

Time entries carry project and cost-code context into review. Forms and safety records keep their supporting evidence and recorded decisions.

  • Time entries carry project and cost-code context.
  • Employee actions connect to reviewer decisions.
  • Supporting evidence stays linked to its record.

External systems and current limits

Payroll exports and reimbursement records support external payment processes. Job-level activity supports review rather than replacing the accounting ledger.

  • Payroll exports support external payroll processing.
  • Job-level activity complements the accounting ledger.
  • Office and Field currently run in an online browser.

Platform details

What is available today?

The reviewed baseline includes timekeeping, time-card review, crew scheduling, employee records, equipment, forms, files and daily-JSA workflows. Some safety master views are read-only. Availability in an installation depends on its permissions, configuration and deployed version.

Does Z-Suite process payroll or reimbursements?

Z-Suite reviews time, closes periods and exports mapped payroll CSV files. Expense workflows retain review and external-payment evidence. The platform does not pay wages, make direct deposits, transfer funds or file payroll taxes.

Can crews use it offline or in a native mobile app?

Office and Field currently require an online browser. Some workflows retain unsent input for retry, but they do not complete work offline or synchronize in the background. Native/offline platform foundations remain planned.

What integrations and languages are supported?

Mapped CSV exports and inbound FOUNDATION setup and reconciliation are implemented. FOUNDATION needs external provisioning and end-to-end validation. These workflows do not provide direct payroll transmission or a general accounting integration catalog. The interface supports English/Spanish selection; help articles are currently English, with Spanish articles planned.

What is being developed or planned?

Planned work includes native/offline foundations, guided help and Spanish articles, optional Microsoft sign-in and permission-controlled read-only AI connections. The accepted native architecture still requires implementation. Daily-JSA Office review, reports, project bundles and connected Equipment/conditions workflows are implemented with the limits described above. Delivery dates for planned work are not set.

How do the workflows connect?

A clocked shift carries project and cost-code context into time-card review, period close and payroll export. Equipment bookings connect assets to shifts or time entries. Forms and JSAs retain their supporting files, recorded content and review history. Role permissions and configured services govern the actions available in each workspace.
