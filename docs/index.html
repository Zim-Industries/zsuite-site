THE PLATFORM TODAY

Work that belongs together.
Finally, in context.

Start with the workflows your team needs today. Keep the people, projects and evidence behind them connected.

01 / TIME

From the first punch
to payroll handoff.

Capture time against projects and cost codes. Give employees a way to review their cards, request corrections and attest to their time—then put the office in control of review and close.

  • Online clock-in/out, breaks and work-allocation changes
  • Individual and bulk time-card approval, returns and corrections
  • Period readiness, locking and mapped payroll CSV exports

Payroll preparation and file handoff—not wage payments, direct deposit or tax filing.

02 / PEOPLE

A plan the crew
can work from.

Bring employee records, crew scheduling and personal schedules into the same operating picture. Compare planned work with clocked attendance without confusing it with approved payroll time.

  • Day, week and month planning with recurring events
  • Employee assignments, shift acknowledgements and time off
  • Crews, positions, employment history and scoped roles

Scheduling is workforce planning, not construction critical-path scheduling or automatic optimization.

03 / EQUIPMENT

More than an
asset list.

Keep the asset record close to its service history and the work it supports. Review requests and operator qualifications, book individual assets and explicitly link equipment to time entries or shifts.

  • Registry, asset history, files, custody and manual locations
  • Maintenance, service, meters, inspections and defects
  • Requests, bookings, operator reviews and work associations
  • Read-only Daily JSA checks on registered assets, linked to saved source revisions

JSA checks require whole-company Equipment and Daily JSA read access; typed unit numbers are not matched. No continuous GPS/telematics, parts inventory or automatic equipment usage costing is implied.

04 / FIELD RECORDS

Capture the work.
Keep the evidence.

Give field information a consistent home: structured forms, project photos and documents, daily reports and expense evidence. Let authorized office reviewers follow the record through its next step.

  • Forms with photos, files, signatures and review controls
  • Office daily reports, coverage views and finalization
  • Shared Files, expenses, per diem and reimbursement handoffs

Uploads depend on configured storage and scanning. Reimbursement evidence does not move money.

05 / SAFETY & TRAINING

Safety conversations.
Recorded context.

Capture daily crew job safety analyses (JSAs), review them in Office and prepare the record for sharing. Keep recorded content, sign-ins and reasoned corrections together—not in disconnected paperwork.

  • Field daily JSA capture, hazards, controls, sketches and sign-ins
  • Office search, record review, managed corrections and late sign-ins
  • Formatted PDF/print and on-demand project bundles of up to 31 days
  • Optional NWS forecast-high and EPA AirNow PM2.5 AQI draft pre-fill; values remain editable
  • Reusable JSA libraries, observations, training and qualification evidence

Bundles cover one project, exclude drafts/archives and require whole-company JSA read or manage access. Lookup needs provider setup, a published site boundary and supported US-provider coverage; manual entry remains available. AirNow requires a key and first-deployment validation, not yet verified by this guide. Some other Office safety masters remain read-only. Records are not regulatory approval or a guarantee of safe work.

Questions worth asking

What is available today?

The time, scheduling, workforce, equipment and field-record workflows described above are implemented in the reviewed software baseline. Some safety masters are read-only, and configuration, permissions and deployment determine availability. The downloadable guide gives the detailed boundaries.

Does Z-Suite process payroll or reimbursements?

No. Time review, period close, mapped payroll CSV exports and reimbursement handoff/evidence workflows prepare and record handoffs. They do not issue wages, transfer funds, make direct deposits or file payroll taxes.

Can crews use it offline or in a native mobile app?

The delivered experience is online Office and Field browser workspaces. Some workflows retain unsent input for explicit retry, but that is not offline completion or background synchronization. Native/offline platform foundations are planned, not a delivered mobile application.

What integrations and languages are supported?

The reviewed product includes mapped CSV export and specific inbound FOUNDATION setup and reconciliation screens. External provisioning and end-to-end operation need validation; no broad accounting integration catalog or direct payroll transmission is implied. English/Spanish interface selection is implemented; the existing help article edition is English, with Spanish articles planned.

What is being developed or planned?

The remaining roadmap covers native/offline foundations, guided help and Spanish articles, optional Microsoft sign-in and permission-controlled read-only AI connections. Daily-JSA Office review, formatted reports, project bundles and connected Equipment/conditions context are now implemented, with the setup and scope limits described above. Native architecture is accepted, but its implementation is still planned. These are not dated commitments; purchase decisions should rely on delivered scope.

What should we validate in a demonstration?

Walk through a clocked shift and correction, period review and your payroll mapping, an asset booking and service record, and a form or JSA. Test the intended roles, sign-in, uploads/scanning and required external connections. Configuration and support terms should be agreed separately.
